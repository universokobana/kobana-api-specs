TOTVS Microsiga Protheus routine for automatic boleto issuance and updating.

:::warning[Boleto Simples]

The modules are currently under the name Boleto Simples and will be migrated and updated gradually.

:::

[TOTVS](https://www.totvs.com) is the leading company in the Brazilian ERP market. It has several management systems, including Protheus.

The integration performs boleto generation from titles registered in accounts receivable and allows automatic payment through scheduled processes.

### Routine Characteristics

- Automatic online transmission (real-time) of accounts receivable titles generated after issuance of output documents, allowing the boleto to be printed and/or sent by email separately to the customer.
- Schedule for receipt and processing of payments and returns received from the bank.
- Interface for monitoring sends, returns, and bank messages (status)
- Interface for boleto resend
- Reports of sends and returns
- Interface for collection instructions (extension, discounts, protests, payments, etc.)
- Sending boleto in the same email as DANFE

### Requirements

- TOTVS Protheus ERP
